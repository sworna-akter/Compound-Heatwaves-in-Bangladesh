
# Rising Heat Extremes in Bangladesh: Trends, Drivers, and the Emergence of Compound Heatwaves

## Research Overview

This research investigates the characteristics, spatial distribution, long-term trends, and climatic drivers of daytime, nighttime, and compound heatwaves across Bangladesh during the pre-monsoon season (March–May), covering 1991–2025.

The study examines the spatiotemporal variations of heatwave characteristics across the eight administrative divisions of Bangladesh and explores the associated atmospheric and land-surface conditions.

## Research Objectives

1.	To study how heatwaves are spread out over time and space in Bangladesh.
2.	To assess the frequency, duration, and trends of heatwaves across the country.
3.	To analyze the climatic factors that affect heatwaves (precipitation and soil moisture, cloud cover, solar radiation, winds).

## Study Area and Period

- **Study area:** Bangladesh
- **Study period:** 1991–2025
- **Season:** Pre-monsoon (March–May)
- **Spatial coverage:** Eight administrative divisions of Bangladesh.

## Data Sources

The research utilizes ERA5 reanalysis datasets and relevant meteorological and land-surface variables, including:

- Near-surface air temperature
- Dewpoint temperature
- Precipitation
- Soil moisture
- Cloud cover
- Surface solar radiation
- Wind components


## Methodology

The research involves:

- Percentile-based heatwave identification.
- Daytime, nighttime, and compound heatwave classification.
- Temporal trend analysis using the Mann–Kendall test and Sen's slope estimator.
- Statistical analysis of heatwave characteristics.
- Regression and inter-variable association analysis.
- Spatial analysis and standardized anomaly visualization.
