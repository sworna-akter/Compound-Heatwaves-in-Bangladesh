
# Research Results

This directory contains selected analytical outputs and visualizations generated during the research.

## Main Research Components

1. Spatial distribution of daytime and nighttime temperatures.
2. Characteristics of daytime, nighttime, and compound heatwaves.
3. Division-wise heatwave distributions.
4. Temporal trends in heatwave characteristics.
5. Climatic anomalies associated with heatwave occurrences.

Selected research figures are available in the `figures/` directory.
